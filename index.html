GeoguessrLineup Challenge

Three minutes on the map. Guess the state, take a player born there, build a traditional NL lineup — then add up the WAR and see how the team fares.

Control →
Host panel. Timer, state picker, player search with stats and bios, random draws, lineup board, final results.
Display →
The OBS overlay. Transparent 1920×1080. Add ?topic=your-topic — or just hit “Copy Display URL” on the control page.
Setup: open Control, type any topic name (add a random tail so nobody else stumbles into it), hit Connect, then Copy Display URL and paste that into an OBS Browser Source at 1920×1080.

Player pool: 6,278 players across 50 states, DC, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands and Guam. Career WAR from Baseball Reference; bios, stats and awards from MLB.
